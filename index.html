项目概述 Project Overview

创作背景 Background

后疫情时代，城市居民的社会孤立与生存不安全感普遍上升。在香港，承载数十年城市记忆的历史唐楼正经历政府资助的翻新，同时面临拆除危机，体现着保存与转型的张力。 The post-pandemic era has witnessed a widespread rise in social isolation and precarity among urban dwellers. In Hong Kong, historic tong lau tenement buildings carrying decades of urban memory are undergoing government-subsidized renovation while facing imminent demolition.

与此同时，由内地来港工作的人才组成的「港漂」群体在本土视觉叙事中仍处于边缘地位，他们的日常焦虑与归属感很少被细腻、真实地呈现。生成式AI视频工具的快速发展为低成本、高效率的叙事内容创作开辟了新的可能。 Meanwhile, the "Hong Kong drifter" group remains underrepresented in local visual narratives. The rapid development of generative AI video tools has opened up new possibilities for low-cost, high-efficiency narrative content production.

创作目标 Creative Objectives

叙事目标 Narrative Objective

讲述三个陌生人从自我封闭到相互支撑的温暖克制故事，全片仅20句台词，大量依靠视觉符号、肢体语言与环境细节传递情绪。 To tell a warm, restrained story of three strangers moving from self-isolation to mutual support, using only 20 lines of dialogue and relying heavily on visual symbols.

美学目标 Aesthetic Objective

构建「新传统唐楼」视觉风格，保留水磨石楼梯、铁闸、木门框等历史质感，同时融入感应灯、电子门禁等现代翻新元素。 To construct a "neo-traditional tong lau" visual style, preserving historic textures while integrating modern renovation elements.

技术目标 Technical Objective

探索一套可控的人文叙事电影AI辅助生产工作流，总结可复用的提示工程方法与问题解决策略。 To explore a controllable AI-assisted production workflow for humanistic narrative films, and summarize reusable prompt engineering methods.

故事与人物 Story & Characters

在中国香港一栋旧楼里，几次看似琐碎的敲门，把三个不擅长求助的人推向彼此。当敲门从催租、争执、维修，变成一种每日确认，门内门外的人都开始改变。 In an old Hong Kong tenement, several trivial knocks push three people who are bad at asking for help towards each other. When knocking becomes a daily confirmation, everyone begins to change.

林越 · 26岁 Lin Yue · 26

被未来困住的人 Trapped by the future

内地来港金融初级分析师，勤奋务实，永远在计算得失。他嘴上说香港机会多，心里真正害怕的是「读了这么多书，最后还是回到原点」。 A junior financial analyst from mainland China, diligent and always calculating gains. What he fears most is ending up back where he started.

陈国强 · 72岁 Chen Guoqiang · 72

被过去困住的人 Trapped by the past

年轻时在内地闯荡，后来孤身返港，在旧楼做了20年保安。他絮絮叨叨讲当年，不是因为风光，而是害怕这一生没人记得。 Wandered in mainland China, returned alone, 20 years as security guard. He talks about old days because he fears no one will remember his life.

苏玉珍 · 74岁 Su Yuzhen · 74

被体面困住的人 Trapped by dignity

香港土生土长，早年留学英国，丈夫早逝，子女定居英国。坐拥几套房产，极其体面孤傲，却把自己活成了无法低头求助的囚徒。 Hong Kong born, UK educated, widowed, children in Britain. She owns properties, proud and dignified, yet a prisoner unable to ask for help.

核心主题：深沉的情感置换 Core Theme: Deep Emotional Transformation

家国不在护照上，在谁愿意在深夜去敲那扇门。新一代外来者林越，通过「叩门」这一行为，无意中承担起对这片土地及其孤独守望者的责任，完成了从「过客」到「守护者」的情感与身份转换。 Home is not on a passport, but in who will knock on the door late at night. Through knocking, Lin Yue inadvertently takes responsibility, transforming from passer-by to guardian.

创作工作流 Creative Workflow

整体制作遵循「人类主导叙事设计 + AI辅助视觉生成 + 人工精修整合」的四阶段工作流 Human-led narrative design + AI-assisted visual generation + manual refinement and integration

01

剧本开发与视觉符号系统 Script & Visual Symbol System

前期由核心创作团队人工完成剧本结构与人物设定。采用三幕剧结构，以三次「敲门」作为推动人物关系的关键节点，同时建立完整的视觉符号系统。 The core creative team completed script and character design manually. We adopted a three-act structure with three "knocking" scenes as key nodes, and built a complete visual symbol system.

使用工具：Microsoft Word（剧本）、Notion（视觉符号整理） Tools: Microsoft Word, Notion

02

分镜拆解与提示工程 Storyboard & Prompt Engineering

将全片拆解为20余个镜头，针对每个镜头进行定向提示工程。核心原则是将文学化的剧本描述转化为结构化的、AI可识别的视觉指令。 We broke down the film into 20+ shots with targeted prompt engineering, transforming literary descriptions into structured, AI-recognizable visual instructions.

标准单镜头提示词五大模块 Five Modules of Standard Shot Prompt

  1. 整体色调风格：画面质感、色温、写实风格Overall tone: texture, color temperature, realistic style
  2. 场景环境：空间布局、材质细节、光源方向Scene: layout, material details, light direction
  3. 人物动作：详细描述动作、表情、状态Character action: movement, expression, state
  4. 镜头语言：景别、运镜、景深、时长Camera: shot size, movement, DOF, duration
  5. 细节约束：背景细节与风格锚点Constraints: background details and style anchors
03

多模型AI生成与迭代筛选 Multi-Model Generation & Screening

采用多模型协同生成策略，匹配不同镜头类型需求。每轮生成后人工筛选可用片段，不合格镜头修改提示词重生成。核心镜头平均迭代3-5次。 Multi-model collaborative strategy matching different shot types. Clips are manually screened after each round. Core shots are iterated 3-5 times on average.

04

后期制作与音画整合 Post-Production & Integration

使用剪映专业版进行剪辑、调色与声音合成。统一全片色调与画质，修正AI生成的局部细节缺陷。预先设计声音框架，以钢琴单音与环境音为核心，加入雨声、敲门声、街声等音效，完成音画同步并输出成片。 Editing, color grading and sound synthesis in CapCut Pro. Unified color tone, corrected AI defects. Sound framework based on piano and ambience, with rain, knock and street sound effects added.

技术挑战与解决方案 Technical Challenges & Solutions

针对小团队独立动画创作中的四大核心技术问题，提出基于工作流的实践性解决方案 Practical workflow-based solutions for four core technical challenges in small-team independent creation

01 跨镜头人物与场景一致性 01 Cross-Shot Consistency

生成式视频模型在连续多镜头叙事中常出现特征漂移：面部变化、服装跳变、材质不统一，破坏叙事沉浸感。 Generative models often suffer from feature drift: facial changes, clothing jumps, material inconsistencies that damage narrative immersion.

  • • 标准化设定手册：统一人物表与场景全景图Standardized handbook: unified character sheets & scene maps
  • • 提示词固定锚点：每镜头开头加入统一风格描述Fixed anchors: unified style description at prompt start
  • • 统一后期修正：全片调色 + 重绘工具微调细节Unified post-production: color grading + inpainting

02 多幕叙事的情绪节奏可控性 02 Controllable Emotional Rhythm

AI生成画面倾向于美化或表情扁平，难以精准匹配从开篇压抑到中段渐暖再到高潮释然的情绪梯度。 AI-generated images tend to be beautified or flat, difficult to match the emotional gradient from depression to warmth to relief.

  • • 情绪分层拆解：5个情绪层级对应固定色温、光质、运镜5 emotional levels with fixed color, light and camera speed
  • • 具体细节替代抽象情绪：用动作与环境传递情绪Concrete details instead of abstract emotion words
  • • 时间流逝自然化：服装、天光、晴雨渐进式设计Progressive time design: clothing, light, weather changes

03 港漂文化细节的隐性呈现 03 Implicit Cultural Details

港漂身份表达易陷入刻板印象，直接文字会打破克制基调；作为背景植入时，AI又常忽略或错误呈现。 Cultural details easily fall into stereotypes; direct text breaks tone; AI often ignores background details.

  • • 背景层：简体招租广告、找换店海报、取件码Background: rental flyers, exchange ads, pickup codes
  • • 中景层：转换插头、双时区时钟、家乡快递Mid-shot: adapters, dual clocks, hometown packages
  • • 特写层：手机家乡壁纸、粤语发音便利贴Close-up: wallpaper, Cantonese pronunciation notes

04 核心剧情反转的精准交付 04 Precise Plot Twist Delivery

核心反转「邮件不是裁员通知，而是心理健康支持」需要呈现苦涩、荒诞、想笑又忍住的复杂情绪，AI易夸张表达。 The twist requires complex bitter-absurd emotion. AI tends to exaggerate expressions and destroy the lingering charm.

  • • 分镜头分步生成：拆分为5个连续镜头分别撰写提示Step-by-step generation: 5 consecutive shots with separate prompts
  • • 明确表演边界：限制微表情幅度，保留东方留白感Clear performance boundaries: limit micro-expressions, retain oriental blank space
  • • 人工多轮筛选：生成10+版本表情特写人工优选Manual screening: 10+ versions, manually selected

学术延伸 Academic Extension

摘自《人机共生：AI时代电影制作实操指南》（徐娴、徐婷 著，北京大学出版社2026）第10章完整案例 Excerpted from Chapter 10 of "Human-AI Symbiosis: Practical Guide to Filmmaking in the AI Era" (Peking University Press, 2026)

关于本书 About the Book

本书不是单纯的提示词工具书，是国内首部「理论+全链路实操+伦理批判」结合的AI电影专著。核心观点：AI不是替代导演，而是作为协作副导演；人类创作者负责叙事决策、审美判断、人文内核，AI负责素材生成、视觉迭代、音频辅助，即「人机共创」。 This is not just a prompt book, but the first Chinese AI film monograph combining theory, full-process practice and ethical critique. Core idea: AI is not a replacement for directors, but a collaborative co-director.

作者：徐娴（岭南大学数码艺术与创意产业系助理教授）、徐婷（资深影视导演） Authors: Xian Xu (Lingnan University), Ting Xu (Senior Film Director)

一 人机共创的核心定位 Core Position of Human-AI Co-Creation

人类主导所有核心创作环节：叙事结构、人物设定、情绪节奏、审美判断、主题表达。AI承担执行性、重复性、试错性工作：视觉素材生成、风格迭代、动作补间、音效合成。创作者的身份从「上帝作者」转向「策展对话者」，在与AI的反复对话中筛选、修正、引导，最终逼近心中的影像。 Humans lead all core creative links: narrative structure, character design, emotional rhythm, aesthetic judgment, theme expression. AI takes on executive, repetitive, trial-and-error tasks.

二 七条人机共创原则 Seven Principles of Human-AI Co-Creation

  1. 叙事优先原则：所有技术手段服务于叙事，不为炫技使用AINarrative first: all techniques serve the story
  2. 人类终审原则：每一轮输出必须经过人工筛选判断，不直接使用AI结果Human final review: every output must be manually screened
  3. 分层控制原则：将创作拆解为不同层级，分别设定AI的参与度与权限Layered control: break down creation into layers with different AI permissions
  4. 细节锚定原则：关键视觉元素必须人工设定锚点，防止AI自由漂移Detail anchoring: key visual elements must be manually set
  5. 情绪具象原则：抽象情绪转化为具体的视觉、听觉细节再输入AIEmotional concreteness: abstract emotions translated into concrete details
  6. 文化内嵌原则：本土文化细节分层植入，避免刻板印象与文化失真Cultural embedding: layered implantation of local cultural details
  7. 伦理自觉原则：全程保持对AI版权、算法偏见、数字伦理的反思Ethical awareness: keep reflecting on copyright, bias and digital ethics

三 东方美学的算法转译路径 Algorithm Translation of Oriental Aesthetics

《叩门》作为本土创作案例，探索了如何将东方美学转化为算法可识别的视觉语言：不直接使用「水墨」「国风」等标签化词汇，而是拆解为具体的视觉参数——低对比度、柔和边界、留白构图、暖金色温、克制表情、缓慢运镜，让AI在写实框架内自然呈现东方叙事的留白与韵味。 As a local creation case, Knock at the Door explores how to translate oriental aesthetics into algorithm-recognizable visual language, dismantled into specific visual parameters.

书中同时指出：当前AI影像的「同质化危机」本质是西方训练数据的审美霸权，本土创作者需要建立自己的视觉语料与提示词体系，走出有文化主体性的AI影像道路。 The book points out that the "homogenization crisis" of AI images is essentially the aesthetic hegemony of Western training data.
