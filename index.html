叩门 Knock at the Door

Knock at the Door A 5-Minute Minimalist Short Film

在中国香港一栋旧楼里，几次看似琐碎的敲门，把三个不擅长求助的人推向彼此。当敲门从催租、争执、维修，变成一种每日确认，门内门外的人都开始改变。 In an old Hong Kong tenement, several trivial knocks push three people who are bad at asking for help towards each other. When knocking becomes a daily confirmation, everyone begins to change.

关于《叩门》 About Knock at the Door

A 5-Minute Minimalist Short Film Centered on Hong Kong Tong Lau and Floating Identity A 5-Minute Minimalist Short Film Centered on Hong Kong Tong Lau and Floating Identity

后疫情时代，城市居民的社会孤立与生存不安全感普遍上升。在香港，承载数十年城市记忆的历史唐楼正经历政府资助的翻新，同时面临拆除危机，体现着保存与转型的张力。 The post-pandemic era has witnessed a widespread rise in social isolation and precarity among urban dwellers. In Hong Kong, historic tong lau tenement buildings carrying decades of urban memory are undergoing government-subsidized renovation while facing imminent demolition.

与此同时，由内地来港工作的人才组成的「港漂」群体在本土视觉叙事中仍处于边缘地位，他们的日常焦虑与归属感很少被细腻、真实地呈现。生成式AI视频工具的快速发展为低成本、高效率的叙事内容创作开辟了新的可能。 Meanwhile, the "Hong Kong drifter" group remains underrepresented in local visual narratives. The rapid development of generative AI video tools has opened up new possibilities for low-cost, high-efficiency narrative content production.

本片入选 SIGGRAPH Asia 2026 Technical Communications，于2026年12月14日至17日在日本东京发表。 This work was accepted to SIGGRAPH Asia 2026 Technical Communications, presented in Tokyo, Japan, December 14-17, 2026.

叙事目标 Narrative Objective

讲述三个陌生人从自我封闭到相互支撑的温暖克制故事，全片仅20句台词，大量依靠视觉符号、肢体语言与环境细节传递情绪。 To tell a warm, restrained story of three strangers moving from self-isolation to mutual support, using only 20 lines of dialogue and relying heavily on visual symbols.

美学目标 Aesthetic Objective

构建「新传统唐楼」视觉风格，保留水磨石楼梯、铁闸、木门框等历史质感，同时融入感应灯、电子门禁等现代翻新元素。 To construct a "neo-traditional tong lau" visual style, preserving historic textures while integrating modern renovation elements.

技术目标 Technical Objective

探索一套可控的人文叙事电影AI辅助生产工作流，总结可复用的提示工程方法与问题解决策略。 To explore a controllable AI-assisted production workflow for humanistic narrative films, and summarize reusable prompt engineering methods.

故事与人物 Story & Characters

三种被困住的人 Three Trapped Souls

They all use their own ways to survive, and ask for help in very awkward ways. They all use their own ways to survive, and ask for help in very awkward ways.

《叩门》的三个人物不是三种"社会议题"的代表，而是三种被困住的人：林越被未来困住，强叔被过去困住，苏玉珍被体面困住。这三个人在中国香港一栋旧楼里相遇。短片不需要解释大的时代背景，只需要让观众看见：他们都在用自己的方式活下去，也都在用很别扭的方式向别人求救。 The three characters are not representatives of "social issues" but three trapped people: Lin Yue is trapped by the future, Uncle Qiang is trapped by the past, and Su Yuzhen is trapped by dignity. They meet in an old Hong Kong tenement. The film needs no grand historical explanation — it only lets the audience see: they all survive in their own ways, and ask for help in very awkward ways.

26

林越 Lin Yue

Junior Financial Analyst
被未来困住的人 Trapped by the Future

内地二三线城市普通工薪家庭出身，通过高才计划留在香港，在中环金融机构做初级分析师。勤奋、务实、永远在算计得失，把"留下来"当作证明自己没有被出身困住的证据。他嘴上说香港机会多，心里真正害怕的是"读了这么多书，最后还是回到原点"。 From an ordinary working-class family in mainland China, staying in Hong Kong through the Talent Scheme, working as a junior analyst in Central. Diligent, pragmatic, always calculating gains and losses. What he fears most is "after all this studying, ending up back where I started."

74

苏玉珍 Su Yuzhen

Madam Su
被体面困住的人 Trapped by Dignity

香港土生土长，早年留学英国，丈夫早逝，子女定居英国。坐拥几套房产，极其体面孤傲，却把自己活成了无法低头求助的囚徒。她拥有很多，却没有能力低头承认自己需要别人。银灰短发整齐，珍珠耳环仍戴着，细金表，瘦削身形，背挺，眼神锐利。 Hong Kong born, UK educated, widowed, children in Britain. She owns properties, proud and dignified, yet a prisoner unable to ask for help. She has much, but cannot lower her head to admit she needs others. Silver-gray hair, pearl earrings, gold watch, sharp eyes.

72

陈国强 Chen Guoqiang

Uncle Qiang
被过去困住的人 Trapped by the Past

年轻时在内地闯荡，后来孤身返港，在旧楼做了20年保安。他絮絮叨叨讲当年，不是因为风光，而是害怕这一生没人记得。灰白色短发，旧式短袖衬衫或薄针织背心，腰间挂着一串大钥匙。虽然岁月在脸上留下了痕迹，但依然能看出年轻时的英俊模样。 Wandered in mainland China when young, returned alone to Hong Kong, 20 years as security guard in the old tenement. He talks about old days not because they were glorious, but because he fears no one will remember his life. Gray-white hair, old-style shirt, a ring of keys at his waist.

门 The Door

Door

门的开启程度直接对应人物的心理距离。从紧闭到虚掩，再到敞开，是三个人关系变化的视觉刻度。 The opening degree of the door directly corresponds to the characters' psychological distance — from closed to ajar to open.

邮件与药盒 Email & Pill Box

Email / Crystal Pill Box

一对镜像符号，分别代表两位主角各自无法言说的压力。林越的工作邮件，苏太的水晶药盒，都是沉默的求救。 A pair of mirrored symbols representing the unspeakable pressure of the two protagonists — Lin Yue's work email and Su Tai's pill box.

阳台 The Balcony

Balcony

全片的情感灯塔，代表苏太对生活的态度。花草、夕阳、木椅——在封闭的唐楼里，阳台是唯一向世界敞开的地方。 The emotional lighthouse of the whole film, representing Su Tai's attitude towards life. Flowers, sunset, a wooden chair — the only open space in the closed tenement.

雨 The Rain

Rain

后疫情压抑氛围的外化，也是情感洗涤的隐喻。从开篇雨夜的冷蓝，到结尾雨过天晴的暖金，雨的变化就是情绪的变化。 The externalization of post-pandemic depression, and a metaphor for emotional cleansing. From cold blue rain to warm golden clearing — the weather mirrors emotion.

创作工作流 Creative Workflow

人机协同的四阶段 Four Phases of Human-AI Collaboration

Human-led narrative design + AI-assisted visual generation + manual refinement and integration. Human-led narrative design + AI-assisted visual generation + manual refinement and integration.

PHASE 01

剧本开发与视觉符号系统设计 Script Development & Visual Symbol System

前期由核心创作团队人工完成剧本结构与人物设定。采用三幕剧结构，以三次「敲门」作为推动人物关系的关键节点，同时建立完整的视觉符号系统。设计「港漂」身份的隐性细节，散入各场景背景以支撑人物行为逻辑，而不打破克制的叙事基调。 The core creative team completed script and character design manually. A three-act structure with three "knocking" scenes as key nodes, plus a complete visual symbol system. Implicit "Hong Kong drifter" identity details scattered into scene backgrounds.

Microsoft Word Notion
PHASE 02

分镜拆解与提示工程 Storyboard Breakdown & Prompt Engineering

将全片拆解为20余个镜头，针对每个镜头进行定向提示工程。核心原则是将文学化的剧本描述转化为结构化的、AI可识别的视觉指令。标准单镜头提示词包含五大模块：整体色调风格、场景环境、人物动作、镜头语言、细节约束。对日常敲门蒙太奇和核心剧情反转等关键段落进行多轮提示词迭代。 The full film broken into 20+ shots with targeted prompt engineering, transforming literary descriptions into structured AI-recognizable visual instructions. Five modules per shot: tone, scene, action, camera, constraints. Multiple rounds of iteration for key sequences.

LLM Prompt Polishing Spreadsheet Management
PHASE 03

多模型AI生成与迭代筛选 Multi-Model AI Generation & Screening

采用多模型协同生成策略，匹配不同镜头类型需求：Midjourney用于概念美术与角色设计，确定核心场景与人物的统一视觉基准；Seedance 2.0用于多数动态镜头，在真实质感与流畅运动方面表现优异；关键长镜头与情绪特写同样由Seedance 2.0补充生成。每轮生成后人工筛选可用片段，核心镜头平均迭代3至5次。 Multi-model collaborative strategy: Midjourney for concept art and character design; Seedance 2.0 for most dynamic shots with realistic texture and smooth motion; key long takes and emotional close-ups also by Seedance 2.0. Manual screening after each round, core shots iterated 3-5 times on average.

Midjourney Seedance 2.0
PHASE 04

后期制作与音画整合 Post-Production & Audiovisual Integration

使用剪映专业版进行剪辑、调色与声音合成。统一全片色调与画质，修正AI生成的局部细节缺陷。预先设计声音框架，以钢琴单音与环境音为核心，按声音节奏匹配剪辑点。最后加入雨声、敲门声、街声等环境音效，完成音画同步并输出成片。 Editing, color grading and sound synthesis in CapCut Pro. Unified color tone and picture quality, corrected AI-generated local detail defects. Sound framework based on piano monophony and ambient sound, with rain, knock and street sound effects added.

技术挑战与解决方案 Technical Challenges & Solutions

小团队独立创作的四大难题 Four Core Challenges in Small-Team Creation

Practical, workflow-based solutions tailored to small-team independent animation creation. Practical, workflow-based solutions tailored to small-team independent animation creation.

01

跨镜头人物与场景一致性 Cross-Shot Character & Scene Consistency

生成式视频模型在连续多镜头叙事中常出现特征漂移：面部变化、服装跳变、材质不统一。苏太的发型、林越的西装风格在不同镜头间容易偏差，唐楼走廊的墙面纹理和门样式难以统一，破坏叙事沉浸感。 Generative models often suffer from feature drift: facial changes, clothing jumps, material inconsistencies. Su Tai's hairstyle and Lin Yue's suit deviate between shots; corridor wall textures and door styles are hard to unify.

  • 标准化设定手册：统一人物表与场景全景图，定义空间布局、材质与光源方向 Standardized handbook: unified character sheets and scene maps with layout, materials and light direction
  • 提示词固定锚点：每镜头开头加入统一风格与场景描述词 Fixed anchors: unified style and scene description at the start of every prompt
  • 统一后期修正：全片调色 + 图像修复工具微调局部细节偏差 Unified post-production: color grading + inpainting for local detail deviations
02

多幕叙事的情绪节奏可控性 Controllable Emotional Rhythm in Multi-Act Narrative

AI生成画面倾向于美化或表情扁平，难以精准匹配从开篇压抑到中段渐暖再到高潮释然的情绪梯度。AI很难自然呈现这种多层次的情绪变化。 AI-generated images tend to be beautified or flat, difficult to match the emotional gradient from depression to warmth to relief. Multi-level emotional changes are hard for AI to present naturally.

  • 情绪分层拆解：5个情绪层级对应固定色温、光质、运镜速度与剪辑节奏 5 emotional levels with fixed color temperature, light hardness, camera speed and editing rhythm
  • 具体细节替代抽象情绪：用黑眼圈、散乱衬衫、外卖盒等具体描述传递压力 Concrete details instead of abstract emotion: dark circles, messy shirt, takeaway boxes
  • 时间流逝自然化：服装轮换、天光偏移、晴雨交替的渐进式设计 Progressive time design: clothing rotation, sky light shift, sunny-rain alternation
03

港漂文化细节的隐性呈现 Implicit Rendering of Hong Kong Drifter Cultural Details

港漂身份表达易陷入刻板印象，直接文字会打破克制基调；作为背景植入时，AI又常忽略或错误呈现。需要在不打断叙事的前提下，让文化细节自然存在于画面中。 Hong Kong drifter identity easily falls into stereotypes; direct text breaks the restrained tone; AI often ignores or misrepresents background details. Cultural details must exist naturally without interrupting narrative.

  • 背景层：简体招租广告、找换店海报、取件码通知 Background layer: Simplified Chinese rental flyers, exchange shop posters, pickup codes
  • 中景层：转换插头、双时区时钟、家乡快递包裹 Mid-shot layer: travel adapters, dual-timezone clocks, hometown packages
  • 特写层：手机家乡壁纸、粤语发音便利贴 Close-up layer: hometown wallpaper, Cantonese pronunciation sticky notes
04

核心剧情反转的精准交付 Precise Delivery of the Core Plot Twist

核心反转「邮件不是裁员通知，而是心理健康支持」需要呈现苦涩、荒诞、想笑又忍住的复杂情绪，AI易夸张表达或破坏余韵。这种微妙的情绪层次是AI生成的最大难点之一。 The core twist — "the email is not a layoff notice, but mental health support" — requires bitter, absurd, suppressed-laughter complex emotion. AI tends to exaggerate or destroy the lingering charm. This subtle emotional layering is one of AI's hardest challenges.

  • 分镜头分步生成：拆分为5个连续镜头分别撰写提示词 Step-by-step generation: split into 5 consecutive shots with separate prompts
  • 明确表演边界：限制微表情幅度，保留东方留白感 Clear performance boundaries: limit micro-expression amplitude, retain oriental blank space
  • 人工多轮筛选：生成10+版本表情特写，人工优选最克制的一版 Manual screening: generate 10+ versions of expression close-ups, manually select the most restrained
